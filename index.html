What is The Ten Rings?

THE TEN RINGS is a parent platform that brings together projects blending technology, life science, green technology, bioinformatics and computational biology.

Each project, or “ring,” tackles a real-world challenge with a computational, evidence-driven approach.

"Rings overlap, connect and strengthen one another. No problem is solved by one field alone, and neither is any ring."

Our Belief

  • Problems are interdisciplinary The best solutions come from combining fields.
  • Data is the new microscope Computation lets us see patterns that experiments alone cannot.
  • Sustainability is not optional Every solution should be kind to people and the planet.
  • Build in the open Ideas improve when they are shared, tested and challenged.

Our Projects

Each project on this site starts with a real problem and ends with a practical, testable idea.

Hariyali 2.0

The problem: Desertification and dropping crop yields in hostile, shifting climates.

Our approach: Engineered soil microbiomes and drought-resistant synthetic plasmids to restore ecological balance.

Rings involved: Bioinformatics · Green Tech · Computational Approaches

Status: In progress

Gutek

The problem: Fragile, heavily polluting petrochemical supply chains for materials.

Our approach: Harnessing heavily edited yeast and bacterial chassis to weave advanced polymers.

Rings involved: Bioinformatics · Green Tech · Computational Approaches

Status: In progress

Project Aegis

The problem: Extreme vulnerability to unknown pathogenic mutations breaching perimeters.

Our approach: Distributed network of synthetic immune-system sensors and sequence analyzers.

Rings involved: Bioinformatics · Computational Biology · Life Science

Status: Idea

How We Work

1. Identify a real-world problem worth solving
2. Research the science and existing solutions
3. Compute by analysing data, building models and testing ideas
4. Design a sustainable, practical approach
5. Share the results openly

Why It Matters

Climate change, antimicrobial resistance, food security and new diseases are not problems for one discipline. They need people who can read DNA and write code, and who understand ecosystems as well as algorithms. THE TEN RINGS is built for that kind of thinking.

Who It’s For

  • Students and early researchers looking for project ideas and collaborators
  • Innovators who want to link science with technology
  • Mentors and institutions interested in interdisciplinary work
  • Anyone curious about how science can solve everyday problems
